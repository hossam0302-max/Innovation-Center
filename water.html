Water STRIP’s Mandate

Water STRIP Mandate

Vision Statement

Advancing sustainable water innovation from Saudi Arabia to the world, setting new standards in efficiency, resilience, and impact

Mission Statement

Driving research and technological innovation through stakeholder collaboration to ensure a sustainable water future for KSA
Value Proposition

Value Proposition Statements

Advance national priorities through R&D and fostering innovation dynamics

Strengthen global water leadership by influencing policy, exporting knowledge, and enhancing industry visibility

Expand opportunities by facilitating access to resources and funding

Develop industry pioneers through training and mentorship

To deliver its mandate, the Water STRIP will have 4 main functions: service coordination, support delivery, internal operations, and marketing & communication

Functions of Water STRIP

Water Strip Functions
Service Coordination
Facilitates alignment around member-led services, maps ecosystem needs, and manages inbound requests from stakeholders.
Delivery Enablement
Oversees execution of STRIP-hosted services, such as knowledge-sharing events, networking forums, and collaboration platforms.
Internal Operations
Manages staffing, internal systems, compliance, and partner coordination to ensure operational continuity.
Marketing & Communication
Drives awareness of STRIP services and positioning through strategic communications, branding, and outreach efforts.
